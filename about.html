About

I'm a machine learning engineer based in Mountain View. I studied mechanical engineering at IIT Bombay, where I also spent time on computer science and data science, and later completed a master's in AI Engineering at Carnegie Mellon.

At work I tend to gravitate toward the boundary between models and systems: inference servers, streaming pipelines, GPU performance, evaluation, and the awkward details that decide whether a model is useful in practice.

Outside my immediate work, I'm interested in biological foundation models and in making experimental science easier to automate.
