Frédéric Chopin · Op. 62 No. 2

Nocturne in E major

There’s a second conversation going on underneath the melody. In the opening it’s easy to hear only the long line on top. On another listen, follow the quieter notes inside the chords and hear how often they change the color of a phrase.

That tension comes to the surface in the restless middle section. The return of the opening tempo is one of the places where taking a little time feels most natural.
